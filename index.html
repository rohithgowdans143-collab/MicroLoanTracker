Features

Everything You Need to Thrive

A complete ecosystem for microfinance — built for India's small vendors and entrepreneurs.

Borrower–Lender Matching

Smart algorithm connects small vendors with verified lenders based on credit score, loan amount, and repayment history.

Aadhaar & Gov ID Verification

Secure identity verification using Aadhaar card, PAN card, and government-approved documents with OTP authentication.

Loan Tracking Dashboard

Real-time tracking of all active loans, EMI schedules, due dates, interest accumulation, and outstanding balances.

Repayment Recording

Log daily/weekly/monthly repayments, generate receipts, and automatically update balances with instant notifications.

Daily Sales Tracking

Record daily sales figures, analyze trends, and use sales data to calculate repayment capacity and creditworthiness.

Smart Notifications

Get SMS and in-app alerts for due dates, payment confirmations, new loan approvals, and overdue reminders.

Process

How MicroLend Works

From registration to repayment — a seamless 4-step journey.

01

Register & Verify

Create your account and verify your identity using Aadhaar card, PAN card, or other government ID. Secure OTP-based confirmation.

02

Apply for Loan

Browse available lenders, compare interest rates, submit loan applications, and get matched based on your profile and needs.

03

Receive Funds

Once approved, funds are directly transferred to your linked bank account. Track the disbursement in real-time on your dashboard.

04

Track & Repay

Log daily sales, make repayments on schedule, and watch your credit score grow. Build a reliable financial history.
